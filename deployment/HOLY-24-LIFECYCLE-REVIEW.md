# Record archive and reference-safe tenant Trash foundation (HOLY-24)

Unexposed service on the mandatory verified suite tenant mapping; no public API/UI activation, live tenant grants or retention policy is created. Archive/unarchive require explicit archive capability and expected versions, preserve IDs/history/references and hide archived records from normal active selectors. Archived records remain readable under current rights and require unarchive before ordinary editing/new linking.

Deletion requires explicit delete capability, a separately reviewed tenant 30-day retention policy, and an actor/workspace-bound five-minute review. Reviews bind record, schema, policy, retention, data and suite-authority revisions. Any incoming internal parent, relationship, document, app registry or custom-record-value association blocks deletion, including inaccessible references. Owned outgoing relationships remain part of the historical record in Trash: this preserves a Contract’s required legal party and an Account’s parent rather than making those records impossible to recover. Referenced targets remain protected even while the referring record is in Trash; no relationship cascade occurs. This owned-relationship distinction needs confirmation in full lifecycle acceptance before activation. The response exposes no hidden IDs, titles or counts. A complete real app-reference inventory remains an activation gate; an empty or incomplete integration registry is not claimed as global proof of absence.

Confirmed deletion enters tenant Trash with actor/time, previous active/archived state and exactly thirty-day deadline. It releases unique-value reservations, emits protected history plus metadata-only audit/outbox, and is idempotent. Restore preserves ID, checks current view/restore rights, schema and typed values, reference authority, uniqueness and retention. It creates no grants. A uniqueness conflict rolls recovery back with the original Trash row intact. Expiry is checked using the database clock and again after the final suite-authority check immediately before commit. At the deadline recovery is denied even if a retention hold preserves underlying storage.

Trash lists expose permitted recoverable metadata only, with fifty-row keysets, actor/tenant/workspace/query/revision-bound five-minute cursors and current collection/record capability unions. Deletion of a saved view remains independent from deletion of records. Permanent removal has no service, route, executable or scheduler here. A separate policy/permission/explicit-action design is needed for any later purge.

Tests cover hidden reference denial, current rights, reviews/expiry, idempotency, archived restoration, thirty-day boundaries including a slow final authority check, uniqueness/schema recovery failures, revoked grant preservation, Trash pagination, metadata privacy and actual PostgreSQL concurrent Trash/restore. Native mobile/keyboard Trash UI, real tenant retention approval, complete application-reference inventory and live role acceptance remain open. This foundation does not mark full HOLY-24 Done.
